# MoQ session analyzer

Media over QUIC (MoQ) moves live media through relays as tracks, groups and objects. Relays and endpoints that log each object they send and receive, as mlog files, let you follow every object across every hop and measure where the time goes.

The [showcase](/showcase/) has real sessions, analyzed and published as plain pages. The [analyzer](/analyzer/) does the same for your own mlog files, entirely in your browser: nothing is uploaded.
